# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Artists, painters, and muralists using the grid method to transfer reference images to physical canvases.

## Product Purpose
To provide a fast, simple way for artists to overlay a measured grid on a reference image and export it for physical transfer. Success means an artist can prep their reference image in seconds without dealing with complex photo editing software.

## Positioning
A hyper-focused, instant client-side utility specifically for grid generation and layout, avoiding the bloat and complexity of full photo editors.

## Operating Context
Used in the studio or on the go, often on a desktop or tablet before starting physical work. Users typically upload an image, adjust the grid to match their physical canvas dimensions, and download the result to print or view on a device while painting.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Fast, client-side only application (no backend).
- Images are processed entirely in the browser.
- Custom and standard paper sizes with adjustable DPI.
- Configurable grid (rows, columns, line color, labels).
- Image drag, resize, and snapping on the canvas.
- Undo/redo support.

## Brand Commitments
- Preserve the name "GridSketch".

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing Next.js web application with canvas rendering.
- Current functional UI for image manipulation and grid settings.
